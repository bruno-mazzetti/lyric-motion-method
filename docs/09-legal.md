# 09 — Rights, licences and disclosure

This is a practical summary by the author of the repo, **not legal advice**. Check each licence yourself before publishing anything.

## What this repository contains and does not contain

| Item | In the repo? | Terms |
|---|---|---|
| Engine, instruments, tools (code) | yes | MIT (`LICENSE`) |
| The reference song | **no** | belongs to its author(s)/rights holders |
| The reference lyrics | **no** | belongs to their author |
| Transcripts, alignment data, scene lists of the reference song | **no** (they contain the lyrics) | — |
| Reference scene table (`src/clip/Full.tsx`) | yes | MIT; it references single keywords of the reference song (e.g. to anchor an animation on a word) but not its text |
| Demo audio | yes | synthetic tone generated by `examples/make_demo.py`; no rights issue |
| Demo words | yes | William Blake, *The Tyger* (1794), public domain |
| Stills in `docs/img/` | yes | frames of the published reference video; the video, song and lyrics are not licensed under the repo's MIT licence |

## Third-party terms to check

- **Remotion** — rendering videos with it is subject to Remotion's own licence, which has conditions depending on who you are and how you use it. Read the current terms at remotion.dev before commercial use or use by a company.
- **Fonts** — Barlow Condensed and Caveat are loaded through `@remotion/google-fonts` (Google Fonts, open-source licences). Fonts are fetched at render time.
- **Whisper** (large-v3 via faster-whisper), **wav2vec2** (torchaudio / whisperx aligner), **librosa**, **PyTorch** — each has its own licence and model terms. Check them for your use.
- **Node/npm dependencies** — see `package-lock.json`.

## Your own song

Only use audio and lyrics you have the right to use. Forced alignment and rendering of someone else's song into a video can be a copyright issue irrespective of this code.

## AI disclosure

The reference video was produced with an AI coding agent writing the code and a human directing, listening and deciding; the song and lyrics came from other sources and are credited in the video. When you publish a video built this way, follow the **current disclosure rules of the platform you publish on** (for example, check YouTube's policy on altered or synthetic content) and credit the contributors honestly.
