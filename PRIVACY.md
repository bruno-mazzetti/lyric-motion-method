# Privacy

This repository's author (Bruno Mazzetti) also runs a small personal backup tool (called "rclone-sauvegarde") that copies the author's own files to the author's own Google Drive account.

- The tool is used by one person only: its author.
- It does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal data.
- It only accesses the author's own Google Drive, and only to copy backup files.
- No data is sent to any third party.
